01 — The beginning

A world without a single path.

Grand World is an open-world MMORPG set in a dark fantasy universe. A world filled with secrets, challenges and places waiting to be discovered.

You decide where to go, what to pursue and how your adventure unfolds. There is no single road you must follow.

Explore at your own pace. Create your own story. Forge your own legend.
