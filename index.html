What is Grand World?

Grand World is an open-world MMORPG with dark fantasy and anime-inspired elements. Explore a huge world, discover hidden secrets, fight enemies, and create your own adventure. There is no single path you have to follow. Explore different regions, take on challenges, and progress at your own pace.
