01 — The beginning

An adventure
of your own.

Grand World is an open-world MMORPG set in a dark fantasy universe. Explore a vast world, discover hidden secrets, face challenges and create your own adventure.

There is no single path you must follow. Travel through different regions, uncover their stories and progress at your own pace. Inspired by the worlds and adventures we love, Grand World aims to create an identity of its own.
