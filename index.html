01 — What is Grand World?

More than a world.
Your own adventure.

Grand World is an open-world MMORPG set in a dark fantasy universe. Explore a huge world, discover hidden secrets, fight enemies, and create your own adventure.

Grand World is non-linear. You are not forced to follow one specific path. Explore different regions, take on challenges, and progress at your own pace.

The world is also inspired by different anime, including series such as Naruto and One Piece. Our goal is to bring together the things people love about these worlds while making Grand World feel like its own unique experience.
